Our contribution

We integrate pretrained models and published editing research into a usable maintenance workflow: conversational interaction, explicit correction approval, scoped retrieval, sources, version history, before/after comparison and withdrawal. The sealed visual study provides a separate, reproducible comparison with raw evidence and documented failures. We do not claim to have invented MEND or trained the foundation models.
