Our contribution

We integrate pretrained models and published editing research into a usable maintenance workflow: conversational interaction, explicit correction approval, scoped retrieval, sources, version history, before/after comparison and withdrawal. The controlled operations study compares two attributed checkpoints and five matched configurations, with split-controlled data, raw answers, uncertainty estimates and failure analysis. The current text reasoner is an attributed upstream checkpoint. Our earlier auxiliary-model training and visual experiments have their own separate evidence.
