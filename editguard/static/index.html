How to read completed scores
Correct: credited by the frozen scoring rules. Incorrect: a supported answer disagrees with the expected fact. Unscored by fixed rules: generated wording was outside the scorer's supported forms or did not establish a complete answer; it receives no accuracy credit. This is a completed scoring outcome, not a queued task. Clarification / abstention: the system did not endorse an answer. Execution error: inference could not complete. Every outcome stays in the accuracy denominator.
The original exports call unscored outputs “pending_review” or “pending_uncredited”; those field names are retained for reproducibility. They do not mean training or final evaluation is still running.
